# Governance and Review Model

## Minimum review gates

1. **Source verification:** confirm that the source is authoritative and record its retrieval date.
2. **Normalization review:** confirm that the obligation accurately reflects source language and retains a pinpoint citation.
3. **Legal-status review:** confirm whether the material is binding, proposed, advisory, voluntary, interpretive, superseded, or withdrawn.
4. **Applicability approval:** approve facts, assumptions, reasoning, decision, and review date.
5. **Control-owner acceptance:** confirm that a named owner accepts responsibility for control design and operation.
6. **Evidence assessment:** determine whether evidence is relevant, authentic, complete, timely, and sufficient.

## Automation boundary

Automation may:

- retrieve public sources;
- calculate hashes;
- extract candidate provisions;
- classify obligation types;
- suggest mappings;
- identify changed text;
- validate schemas;
- generate review queues.

Automation must not silently:

- convert proposals into binding requirements;
- approve legal interpretations;
- invent citations;
- mark controls effective without evidence;
- overwrite the append-only ledger;
- suppress conflicting evidence.
